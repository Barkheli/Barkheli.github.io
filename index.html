बाउन्न पत्तीको त्रिपन्न खेल · Teen Patti, Call Break and Marriage

Play the card games of Nepal and India against friendly computer rivals, anywhere, with play chips only. There's also a Marriage score calculator for your real games with friends.

Questions

Can I win or buy real money?
No. Chips are play money only and have no value outside the game. Nothing can be bought, sold or cashed out.
I ran out of chips.
Tap the + next to your chips: collect the daily bonus, take the free refill when you are low, or watch a short ad for free chips.
Why did I lose chips when I came back?
Leaving a Call Break game or Marriage deal before it ends counts as a loss, as at a real table.
How do I change my character, sound or vibration?
Open Settings (the gear at the top right of the home screen).
How do I change my ad choices?
See the privacy policy. On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. In the EU and UK: the app's Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
Does it work offline?
Yes. Games are played against computer rivals on your phone. Ads need a connection, the game does not.
