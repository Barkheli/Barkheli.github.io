Privacy Policy

Ace बादशाह · Effective 14 November 2026

Ace बादशाह ("the app") is a card game with Teen Patti, Call Break and Marriage, played against computer opponents, plus a Marriage score calculator. It is made by an independent developer, Surendra Tamang ("we"). This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in, no name, email or phone number is ever asked for.
  • Your game progress (chips, level, stats, settings) is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information, including the advertising ID, to show and measure ads. You can limit this.
  • Chips are play money only. They cannot be bought, sold, won or exchanged for real money or prizes.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your chips, level and experience, game statistics, your chosen character, your settings (sound, vibration, motion) and a game in progress. This stays on your phone in the app's own storage. We do not receive it and cannot see it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, the app shows full-screen ads between rounds and optional "watch an ad for free chips" ads, provided by Google AdMob. To deliver, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android) or IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking)Showing and measuring ads, limiting how often you see the same ad
IP address, approximate location derived from itShowing ads suitable for your region, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (ads shown, taps, how long a video was watched)Measuring ads, giving the reward for rewarded ads
Diagnostics about the ads SDKKeeping ads working correctly

This information is handled by Google under its own policy: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. We do not sell personal information and we do not receive any information that identifies you.

Your choices

  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks whether to allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the IDFA is not shared and you see less personalised ads. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can delete or reset your advertising ID in Settings → Privacy → Ads (or Google → Ads).
  • European Economic Area, United Kingdom and Switzerland: the app shows Google's consent form before any personalised ads. You can change your choice later from the app's Settings → "Ad privacy choices".
  • The game works the same whatever you choose. Only the kind of ads changes.

Children

The app contains simulated gambling (play-money card games) and is intended for adults: it is rated 18+ on the App Store and for mature audiences on Google Play. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Ads in the app are limited to content rated for teens and older.

No real-money gambling

The app does not offer real-money gambling or any chance to win money or prizes. Chips have no monetary value and cannot be purchased or cashed out. Practice or success in this game does not imply future success at real-money gambling.

Security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Advertising data collected by Google is kept according to Google's retention policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
